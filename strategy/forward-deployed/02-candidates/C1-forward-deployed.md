# C1 — Forward Deployed: the craft of the role

## Premise
A channel about what a forward deployed engineer actually does: embedding with a
customer, finding where AI moves the needle, shipping it, and handing it over. The
role is the hot job title of 2026 — Palantir originated it, the frontier labs have
made it aspirational — and there is no established YouTube voice explaining it from
the inside.

## Core loop
Take one facet of the role (discovery, scoping, the first two weeks, handover,
saying no) and explain it with a worked example.

## Who watches
Senior engineers considering the move into FDE work. Some engineering managers
trying to work out whether to hire one. Recruiters.

## Can they buy?
**Mostly no.** Job seekers have no budget. The engineering managers in the audience
are a minority but they are the right minority.

## Receipts spent
R7, R8, R14, R15 — heavy on the career narrative, which is the finite resource.

## NDA route
A, mostly. The role's texture comes from client engagements, so the strongest
anecdotes are the least tellable. This is a real structural weakness.

## Production cost
Low. 4–5 hours. Largely talking head, minimal build.

## Discovery mechanism
Search on career terms, plus recommendation from adjacent career content.

## Bridge asset
Weak. A CV template or interview guide attracts exactly the wrong audience.

## Kill condition
If, after six episodes, inbound is dominated by people asking for career advice or
referrals rather than engagements, this candidate has been proven to serve the
secondary goal at the cost of the primary one.

## Note
The **name** may be worth keeping even if the **content** is rejected. See
ADR-004 — channel identity and episode topic are separable decisions.
