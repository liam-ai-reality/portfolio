# C5 — Building Reality AI in public

## Premise
The founder journey. Building the consultancy and its products in the open — wins,
losses, decisions, numbers.

## Core loop
Periodic update on what happened, what was learned, what is next.

## Who watches
Other founders and aspiring founders. Some peers. Very little overlap with buyers.

## Can they buy?
**Rarely.** Founders of small companies are not the buyer profile for enterprise AI
deployment work.

## Receipts spent
R8 partially, but the format demands *current* numbers, and Reality AI's revenue is
not public and should not become public.

## NDA route
Fails the gate frequently. Client work is the substance of the business, so an honest
build-in-public account either breaches confidentiality or is hollowed out to the
point of being uninteresting. There is no stable middle position.

## Production cost
Low. 3–4 hours.

## Discovery mechanism
Almost none. Build-in-public works on LinkedIn and X, where the feed carries it. On
YouTube there is no search intent for an unknown person's business update, and the
recommendation system will not surface it without an existing audience.

## Bridge asset
None.

## Kill condition
n/a — rejected before scoring is meaningful, though it is scored anyway for completeness.

## Verdict
**Reject.** The right home for this material is LinkedIn, where Liam's network
already is and where the format actually works. It should not consume a video slot.
