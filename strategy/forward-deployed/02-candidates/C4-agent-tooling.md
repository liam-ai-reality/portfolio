# C4 — Agent tooling: keeping AI coding agents honest

## Premise
Content for developers using Claude Code, Cursor and Aider, from the person who
builds the guardrails. klasp's natural audience. The counter-position to the
prevailing "10x your output" register: quality gates, commit-time enforcement,
review discipline, agent conformance.

## Core loop
Take a failure mode of agent-written code, demonstrate it, then demonstrate the gate
that catches it.

## Who watches
Working developers. Large, fast-growing, highly engaged audience — by some distance
the biggest available here.

## Can they buy?
**No.** Individual contributors do not buy consulting engagements. Some fraction
become advocates inside companies that do, but that is a slow and indirect path,
and it is not what the primary goal asks for.

## Receipts spent
R1, R3, R4. All Tier 1, all renewable. Cheap in credibility terms.

## NDA route
B, entirely. Own material.

## Production cost
Low-medium. 4–6 hours. The tooling exists and demos itself.

## Discovery mechanism
Search plus recommendation. Highest raw volume of any candidate — and the most
competition, sitting adjacent to the saturated practical-builder tier identified
in the market read.

## Bridge asset
`klasp` itself. Excellent for OSS adoption, close to worthless for consulting
inbound — an install is not a lead.

## Kill condition
Already conditionally failed: the goals document (`00-context/04-goals.md`) ranks
OSS adoption tertiary, and this candidate cannot serve the primary goal. It survives
only as an occasional series, justified by reach and by keeping klasp alive.

## Note
This is the candidate most likely to succeed *as a YouTube channel* and least likely
to succeed *as a business asset*. Do not let subscriber growth here pull the channel
off course — that is the specific failure mode to watch for, and it will be tempting
precisely because the numbers will look good.
