# Goals

Decided 2026-07-30: **both, consulting first.**

## Primary — consulting inbound for Reality AI

The channel exists to make qualified buyers arrive already convinced. B2B buyers
typically watch several videos before making contact, and those are found through
search, before they know the name attached.

**What success looks like at 6 episodes:** two or more inbound conversations that
cite a specific video, from someone with budget authority.

**What success does not look like:** subscriber count, view count, watch hours,
or YouTube ad revenue. Ad revenue on this channel will be negligible and chasing it
would actively damage the primary goal by pulling topics toward volume.

## Secondary — FDE role optionality

The portfolio says "Open to AI Forward Deployed Engineer roles." A channel that
demonstrates enterprise-deployment judgement serves this at close to zero marginal
cost, because it is the same evidence a buyer wants.

**Where they conflict:** career-advice content ("how to become an FDE", salary,
interview prep) serves the secondary goal and actively harms the primary one by
filling the audience with people who cannot buy. When in doubt, make the video for
the buyer.

## Tertiary — klasp and OSS adoption

Nice to have. Dev-tooling content reaches the largest audience and the least
commercially useful one. Keep it occasional and treat installs as a vanity-adjacent
metric, not a goal.

## Explicit non-goals

- YouTube Partner Programme revenue
- Sponsorships (they would compromise the teardown format's credibility)
- Course or info-product sales
- Becoming a full-time creator

## The economic logic

A single enterprise engagement is worth more than any plausible ad revenue this
channel could generate in years. A video with 900 views that produces two qualified
calls beats one with 40,000 views that produces none. Every format, topic and
thumbnail decision downstream should be tested against that sentence.
