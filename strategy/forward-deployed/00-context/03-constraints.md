# Constraints

The plan has to survive these. Most channel strategies fail on constraint 1.

## C-1 — Billable hours are the real cost

Every hour on the channel is an hour not billed. A weekly polished long-form is
roughly 8–12 hours a week once you count scripting, building whatever is being
demonstrated, shooting, editing and thumbnails. That is a part-time job and it will
lose to client work every single time there is a deadline.

**Implication:** fortnightly, batched into two shoot days a month, in finite seasons.
Anything that requires sustained weekly output is rejected at the design stage, not
abandoned in month three.

## C-2 — Most of the best material is under NDA

The portfolio says "client specifics abstracted" on five of nine projects. The
richest stories — what actually went wrong at a named media company — are exactly
the ones that cannot be told. See `05-nda-boundaries.md` for the routes through.

**Implication:** formats that depend on war stories are structurally fragile.
Formats built on public material are structurally safe. This is a heavy thumb on
the scale in stage 03.

## C-3 — Two audiences, one channel

Consulting buyers (CTO, VP Eng, Head of AI, compliance lead) and hiring managers at
labs want overlapping but distinct things. Buyers want to see judgement about *their*
problem. Hiring managers want to see depth of craft.

**Implication:** the overlap is real — both respond to "shows evidence of shipping
into hard environments" — but when they conflict, consulting wins. See ADR-001.

## C-4 — Zero audience, zero name recognition

No subscribers, no mailing list, no prior video presence. Nothing will be carried by
personality or reputation for at least six months.

**Implication:** season 1 is entirely search- and hook-anchored. Personality-led
formats (vlogs, build-in-public, opinion monologues) are deferred until there is an
audience to hold them.

## C-5 — Solo production

No editor, no producer, no thumbnail designer, at least initially.

**Implication:** the visual identity must be cheap to produce *by design* — terminal,
editor, console, real artefacts. This constraint and the anti-slop tailwind point the
same direction, which is lucky.

## C-6 — UK/EU base

London. GMT/BST. Most AI creator gravity and most US buyer attention is 5–8 hours behind.

**Implication:** not a real handicap for asynchronous long-form, and the EU regulatory
angle turns the location into an asset rather than a liability.

## C-7 — Face on camera (decided)

Confirmed: face + screen. Removes the anonymity problem the January 2026 slop purge
created for faceless channels, but raises per-episode production cost and means
episodes cannot be quietly outsourced later.
