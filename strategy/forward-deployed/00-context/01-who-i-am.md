model calls, API, UI, production ops — and leave documentation their team can own.

## The positioning line I already use

> I deploy and integrate frontier models; I do not train them. If you need novel
> model research, I am not your hire. If you need someone to embed with a customer
> and ship that research into production they actually use, I am.

This is the channel's thesis in two sentences. It is a **counter-position**: it
concedes the glamorous half of the market and claims the half that has the budget.
Do not dilute it by drifting into model-research commentary.

## What I am not

- Not a researcher. Not going to explain transformer internals.
- Not a generalist tech commentator. No hardware reviews, no "top 10 AI tools".
- Not a full-time creator. This is a top-of-funnel asset for a consulting business.

## Voice

Direct. Numbers over adjectives. Willing to say a popular thing is wrong, and
willing to show my own failures — the failures are the credential. British,
understated, no hype register. The portfolio already has a "Straight talk" section;
