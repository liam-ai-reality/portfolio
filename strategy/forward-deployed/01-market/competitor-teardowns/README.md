# Competitor teardowns

Empty by design. Populate before planning season 2 — season 1 is anchored on the
market read and the receipts inventory, not on imitation.

## What to record per teardown

One file per channel, `<handle>.md`:

- Channel, approximate subscriber count, date observed
- **Business model behind the channel** — this is the point. Ads? Course? Agency?
  SaaS? Job? If you cannot identify it, the channel is probably not a useful comparable.
- Upload cadence and apparent production cost per video
- Title and thumbnail patterns — extract the formula, not the examples
- What is in the description and pinned comment — the bridge asset lives here
- Their three best-performing videos and the hypothesis for why
- Their three worst — usually more informative
- **What they cannot do that Liam can**, and vice versa

## Selection criteria

Pick for *comparable business model*, not comparable topic. The most useful
comparable is a technical creator with a small audience and a high-value B2B
business behind it — that is the shape being aimed at here. The least useful is a
large channel monetised by ads, because none of its incentives transfer.

Target 6–8 teardowns. More is procrastination.
