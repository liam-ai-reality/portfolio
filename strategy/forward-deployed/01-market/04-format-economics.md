# Format economics

## The two revenue models, and which one applies

**YouTube's model:** long-form takes 55% of net ad revenue; Shorts take 45% of a
pooled regional share at roughly $0.01–0.07 RPM. At a realistic year-one scale for
this channel — call it 50k cumulative long-form views — ad revenue is in the low
hundreds of pounds. It is a rounding error.

**This channel's model:** one enterprise engagement. The entire ad-revenue question
is therefore irrelevant to every decision in this repo, and should be actively
resisted whenever it starts influencing topic choice, title writing or length.

**Consequence:** the Partner Programme threshold is not a milestone. Do not optimise
toward it. Do not celebrate it.

## Length

Long-form, 12–20 minutes. Rationale:

- Long enough to demonstrate real work and earn trust from a buyer.
- Short enough to be finished by a busy CTO.
- Below ~8 minutes there is no room to show anything real; above ~25 the completion
  rate collapses for a channel with no established relationship with the viewer.

Do not pad to hit a mid-roll threshold. See above.

## Shorts

Purpose: discovery feeder only. Dual-format posting grows subscribers roughly 3x
faster, which is worth having, but the Shorts algorithm is entirely separate and
optimises swipe-through, loop rate and early engagement — properties of packaging,
not substance.

Rule: **3 Shorts cut from each long-form, zero authored from scratch.** The cut
points are chosen at script time, not found in the edit — mark them in the script
so the shoot covers them cleanly.

Best cut candidates: the moment something breaks, a surprising number stated flatly,
and a one-sentence contrarian claim.

## Cadence

Fortnightly long-form. Two shoot days a month, batched. Six-episode seasons with a
hard review gate.

Why seasons rather than perpetual cadence: a consultant's availability is lumpy, and
an open-ended commitment converts a missed week into a sense of failure and then into
abandonment. A season that ends on purpose can be followed by a gap that is also on
purpose. It also forces the review gate in `08-measure/` to actually happen.

## Cost per episode

Target: **6–8 hours all-in**, including whatever is being built or broken on screen.

If an episode concept cannot be made in 8 hours it is either too ambitious or the
wrong format. Split it or cut it — do not absorb the overrun, because constraint C-1
means the overrun is paid for out of billable time and that is how the channel dies.
