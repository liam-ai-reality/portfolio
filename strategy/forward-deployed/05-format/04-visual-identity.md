# Visual identity

The identity is a direct answer to the January 2026 slop purge: everything on screen
should be checkably real, and cheap to produce because it is real.

## The rule

**Nothing on screen that isn't a real artefact.** Real terminal, real editor, real
AWS console, real diff, real failing CI, real cost curve from real numbers.

**Banned outright:** stock B-roll, robot imagery, glowing brains, neural-network
animations, generic "AI" motion graphics, animated code that isn't real code, and
any generated imagery. Not because they're tacky — because they are the visual
grammar of the content this channel is positioned against, and one shot of it
undoes the differentiation.

This is the rare case where the cheapest option and the strongest option are the
same one (C-5).

## Screen

- Terminal and editor at a size legible on a phone. Test at 360px wide before shooting.
- Real repos, real output, real errors. **Leave the failures in.** A command that
  errors and gets fixed on camera is worth more than a clean take — it is the single
  most efficient proof of humanity available.
- No speed-ramped typing montages.

## Camera

- Face for the open, then picture-in-picture over screen. Face full-frame for the
  organisational and opinion beats.
- Any room that looks like a room someone works in. Not a studio, not a bookshelf
  arranged for the shot.
- Audio matters more than video. A good microphone is the single highest-return
  purchase; everything else can stay as it is.

## Graphics

- Architecture diagrams drawn plainly — boxes, arrows, labels. Excalidraw-register,
  not marketing-register.
- Numbers on screen as plain text over the artefact they came from.
- One accent colour, taken from `assets/tokens.css` at liam.dev so channel and site
  reinforce each other.

## Pace

Deliberate. The audience is senior and is watching to evaluate judgement, not to be
entertained. Jump cuts to remove dead air, never to manufacture energy. No music
under the technical segments.

## The test

Could a viewer reproduce what they just watched? If not, it wasn't real enough.
