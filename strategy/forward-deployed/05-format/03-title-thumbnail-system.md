# Titles and thumbnails

## Titles

The audience is technical and senior. Hype register actively repels them — this is a
segment where restraint outperforms, because overstatement is the marker of the
content they've learned to skip.

### Patterns that fit

**The counted failure** — specific, checkable, no adjective doing the work.
> I ran a viral AI agent through an enterprise security review. It failed 9 checks.

**The cost reveal** — a number a buyer's budget cares about.
> Your AI pilot costs 40x more at 10,000 users than at 10.

**The obligation** — plain, searchable, no cleverness. For Regulated, the search term
*is* the title.
> Writing EU AI Act Annex IV documentation, as an engineer

**The organisational truth** — the thing only a practitioner would say.
> Your AI pilot didn't die in engineering. It died in procurement.

### Rules

- Numbers beat adjectives. "9 checks" beats "shocking".
- Front-load the searchable term — mobile truncates around 50 characters.
- No ALL CAPS, no "INSANE", no "nobody is talking about this", no "you won't believe".
- No question-mark clickbait. A question title implies you might not answer it.
- For Regulated: **the search term is the title.** Do not be creative. Someone typing
  "annex iv technical documentation" must see those words.
- Never overstate the finding. If it failed 6 checks, the title says 6. The audience
  will check, and being caught inflating once costs more than every click it bought.

## Thumbnails

**Cheap by design** (C-5, solo production) and anti-slop by construction.

### The system

- **Real artefacts only.** A terminal with real failing output. A cost curve. A diff.
  A red CI badge. An architecture box that is actually wrong.
- **Face in roughly a third of them**, not all. Face for the organisational and
  opinion episodes; artefact-only for the technical teardowns.
- **One number or three words maximum.** `9 FAILED`. `£40k/mo`. `ANNEX IV`.
- **Consistent palette**, taken from the liam.dev tokens so channel and site match.
- **No stock imagery. No robots. No glowing brains. No arrows pointing at a shocked
  face.** These are the visual grammar of exactly the content this channel is
  positioned against.

### Test

At 210px wide on a phone: is the number legible, and is it obvious a human made a
specific decision? If not, remake it. Everything else is secondary.

### Production

Build one template with slots — artefact screenshot, number, optional face cutout —
and reuse it. Thumbnail time budget: 20 minutes. If it takes longer the template is
wrong.
