# Production runbook

Two shoot days a month, six-episode seasons (ADR-003). Target 6–8 hours per episode;
episode 1 will take 15–20 and that is expected.

## Monthly rhythm

| When | What | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Signal + script both episodes | 4 |
| Week 2 | **Shoot day 1** — build/break work + record ep A | 5 |
| Week 3 | Edit A, publish A, cut Shorts | 3 |
| Week 4 | **Shoot day 2** — record ep B; edit, publish, cut | 6 |

~18 hours a month for two episodes. **Defend the two shoot days in the calendar like
client work.** If they get eaten, the season slips as a unit — it does not degrade
episode by episode, because that is how cadence quietly dies.

## Per-episode sequence

1. **Signal** — run the demand method (or record the artefact's view count). 20 min.
2. **NDA gate** — five questions. 5 min. Fail = reject, not soften.
3. **Script** — fill the template. Hook verbatim, beats, Shorts cut points marked. 90 min.
4. **Build** — do the actual work being shown. The variable cost; this is what overruns.
5. **Record** — screen and camera. Leave the failures in. 60–90 min for 15 min final.
6. **Edit** — cut dead air, no manufactured energy. 90–120 min.
7. **Shorts** — pull the three marked cut points. 30 min.
8. **Package** — title, thumbnail (20 min cap), description, UTM, bridge asset link.
9. **Publish + log** — record actual hours. ADR-005 depends on the number being true.

## Guardrails

- **8-hour ceiling.** At 8 hours, ship what exists or cut scope. Do not absorb the
  overrun — it is paid from billable time, which is how the channel dies (C-1).
- **The build is the risk.** Timebox it separately. If the build isn't done at its
  timebox, the episode becomes "here's how far I got and why it's harder than it
  looks" — which is frequently a better episode anyway.
- **Never publish an episode that failed the NDA gate**, however good it is.
- **One Tier-2 receipt per episode**, and never the same opener twice running.

## Batching notes

Record both episodes' talking-head segments on the same day if energy allows — same
lighting, same shirt continuity is irrelevant across a fortnight gap, and the setup
cost is the expensive part.

Keep a running "found while building" file. Most of season 2's backlog will come from
things that broke during season 1's builds.
