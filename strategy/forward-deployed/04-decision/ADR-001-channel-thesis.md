# ADR-001 — Channel thesis

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-30
- **Deciders:** Liam McCarthy
- **Supersedes:** —

## Context

Liam is starting from zero audience with two goals — consulting inbound for Reality
AI (primary) and FDE role optionality (secondary) — under a hard constraint that
every channel hour is an unbilled hour. Stages 00–03 established the receipts, the
constraints, the July 2026 market, six candidate concepts and a weighted debate.

## Decision

**One channel, not several.** Name: **Forward Deployed**. One thesis, stated as the
channel's promise:

> What actually happens when AI meets a real company.

The channel is positioned on the deployment gap: everyone covers how to *build* AI;
almost nobody covers what breaks when it meets tenancy, authorisation, cost at scale,
audit, procurement, a regulator and a user who doesn't want it. That gap is Liam's
entire working life and it is under-served (`01-market/02-niche-map.md`).

Consulting inbound is the primary metric. Where the two goals conflict, the buyer
wins (`00-context/04-goals.md`).

## Why one channel

- **C-1.** Two channels is two production pipelines against a budget that barely
  supports one.
- **C-4.** A tiny audience split in two is two dead channels.
- The brand through-line — the person who ships AI into real companies — is stronger
  undivided, and it is the thing being sold.

A second channel is earned, not planned. The only clean future split is developer
audience versus compliance buyer, and if that happens the compliance channel should
be **Reality AI-branded, not personal**. Revisit no earlier than the season 3 gate,
and only via a new ADR.

## Consequences

- Topics that don't serve the thesis get rejected even when they'd perform. Model
  launch reactions, tool round-ups and research explainers are out permanently.
- The channel will grow slowly. Accepted — see ADR-005 for what is measured instead.
- Liam is on camera, which means episodes cannot later be outsourced. Accepted
  deliberately: the January 2026 slop purge made a visible practitioner the cheapest
  available differentiator, and it caught faceless human creators in the crossfire.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Several channels from the start** — violates C-1 and C-4.
- **A generalist AI channel** — largest audience, lowest buyer proximity, saturated,
  and competing with full-time creators on production value.
- **Making the FDE role the thesis** — serves the secondary goal at the cost of the
  primary. See ADR-004 for how the name is kept without the topic.
