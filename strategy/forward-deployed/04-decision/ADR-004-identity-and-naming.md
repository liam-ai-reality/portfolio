# ADR-004 — Identity and naming

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-30
- **Deciders:** Liam McCarthy
- **Supersedes:** —

## Context

C1 was rejected as content because career-advice topics attract an audience with no
budget. But the *phrase* "forward deployed" tested as the strongest identity asset
available: Liam already uses it as his tagline, it is his actual job title, the term
carries currency in 2026, and no YouTube channel owns it.

Channel identity and episode topic are separable decisions. Rejecting the topic does
not require rejecting the name.

## Decision

- **Channel name:** `Forward Deployed — Liam McCarthy`
- **Handle:** `@forwarddeployed` (fall back to `@forwarddeployedeng` if taken)
- **Tagline:** *What actually happens when AI meets a real company.*

The name is a **promise about method**, not a topic label: someone who goes into real
organisations and ships. It survives every topic change the channel will ever make,
reads correctly to both buyers and hiring managers, and matches the existing personal
brand at liam.dev.

Liam's name stays in the channel title because the secondary goal is being hired, and
a hiring manager needs to connect the channel to a person and a CV.

## Consequences

- The name must not be allowed to drift the content back toward career advice. The
  channel is named after what Liam *does*, not after the job market for it. If
  "how to become an FDE" appears in the backlog, ADR-002 rejects it.
- Consistent with the portfolio's existing four-lens cockpit framing, so channel,
  site and CV reinforce each other.
- Handle availability is unverified — check before committing anywhere public.

## Rejected alternatives

- **"Liam McCarthy"** — a personal name channel has no promise attached and gives a
  cold viewer no reason to click.
- **"Production Grade" / "The Deployment Layer" / "Ship Mode"** — descriptive, but
  generic, unrelated to the existing brand, and each is one search away from someone
  else's product.
- **A Reality AI-branded channel** — company channels underperform personal ones for
  trust-based B2B, and it would forfeit the secondary hiring goal entirely. Keep in
  reserve for a future compliance-specific spin-out (ADR-001).
