# ADR-003 — Format and cadence

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-30
- **Deciders:** Liam McCarthy
- **Supersedes:** —

## Context

Constraint C-1 — billable hours are the real cost — is the constraint that actually
kills channels. Format and cadence are where it either gets respected or ignored.

## Decision

**Long-form, 12–20 minutes, fortnightly, batched into two shoot days a month, in
seasons of six.**

- **Face + screen.** Talking-head open, then screen-led with picture-in-picture.
- **Shorts: three cut from each long-form, zero authored separately.** Cut points
  marked at script time so the shoot covers them cleanly. Dual-format posting grows
  subscribers ~3x faster; authoring Shorts separately would violate C-1 for a pooled
  RPM of $0.01–0.07.
- **Budget: 6–8 hours per episode all-in**, including the build. An episode that
  can't be made in 8 hours is too ambitious or the wrong format — split it or cut it.
  Episode 1 is expected to take 15–20 hours; that is planned for, not a surprise.
- **Seasons end on purpose.** Six episodes, then a hard review gate (ADR-005), then a
  deliberate gap if wanted.

## Why seasons rather than perpetual cadence

A consultant's availability is lumpy. An open-ended commitment converts a missed week
into a sense of failure and then into abandonment — the single most common way
channels like this die. A season that ends by design can be followed by a gap that is
also by design. It also forces the review gate to actually happen, rather than being
perpetually deferred by the next upload.

## Consequences

- Twelve episodes a year, not fifty. Slow by creator standards, correct by
  consulting-business standards.
- Batching means two dedicated days a month must be defended in the calendar like
  client work. If they get eaten, the season slips as a unit rather than degrading
  episode by episode.
- No mid-roll padding, no thumbnail-chasing, no Partner Programme milestone. Ad
  revenue is a rounding error against one engagement (`01-market/04-format-economics.md`).

## Rejected alternatives

- **Weekly long-form** — 8–12 hours a week. A part-time job that loses to client work
  at the first deadline.
- **Shorts-first** — separate algorithm, negligible revenue, and the wrong audience
  for a consulting funnel.
- **Screen-only, no face** — cheaper, but forfeits the strongest available
  differentiator in the post-slop market, and the January 2026 purge caught faceless
  human creators as collateral damage.
- **Perpetual cadence** — see above.
